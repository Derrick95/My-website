Policies & Framework

Lodging Framework
& Rules

Flexible Layouts

Fully serviced 1, 2, 4, and 5-bedroom luxury plans matching varying group sizes and executive configurations — from solo travellers to large delegations.

Pricing Model

Standardised rates from Ksh 5,000 to Ksh 10,000 per night based on property size, location, and total occupancy level.

Booking Window

Minimum 2-night stay required. Bookings must be confirmed at least 1 week in advance. Last-minute requests subject to availability.

Payment

M-Pesa, bank transfer, or card accepted. A 50% deposit secures your reservation. Balance due upon check-in.

Cancellation Policy

Full refund available with 72 hours notice. Cancellations within 24 hours of check-in are non-refundable.

House Rules

No parties or events without prior approval. Pets by arrangement only. Check-in 2 PM, Check-out 11 AM. Quiet hours 10 PM – 7 AM.
